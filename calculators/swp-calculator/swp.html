What is an SWP Calculator?

A SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan) Calculator is a financial tool used to estimate how much money you can withdraw regularly from your mutual fund investments while the remaining balance continues to earn returns. It is essentially the opposite of SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), where instead of investing regularly, you withdraw a fixed amount periodically (monthly/quarterly).

This calculator is especially useful for individuals who want a steady income stream, such as retirees. By using an SWP calculator, you can plan how much you can withdraw without exhausting your investment too quickly. It helps balance withdrawals and growth to ensure long-term financial stability.

The SWP calculator works on the principle of compounding with withdrawals. Even after withdrawing money, the remaining investment continues to grow based on the expected rate of return. The calculator takes into account the initial investment, withdrawal amount, rate of return, and duration to estimate how long your funds will last.

One key advantage of SWP is flexibility. You can choose the withdrawal amount and frequency based on your needs. The calculator allows you to experiment with different scenarios—like increasing or decreasing withdrawal amounts—to see how it impacts your remaining balance and investment lifespan

How does SWP Calculator Work?

Our SWP Calculator online takes Five main factors into account:

  1. FV (Future Value) → Remaining investment value after withdrawals
  2. P (Principal) → Initial investment amount
  3. r (Rate of Return) → Periodic return rate (monthly)
  4. n (Number of Periods) → Total number of withdrawals (months)
  5. W (Withdrawal Amount) → Fixed amount withdrawn each period

Overall, an SWP calculator is a powerful retirement and income planning tool. It helps you maintain a regular cash flow while ensuring that your investments continue to grow, giving you financial confidence and control over your future.
